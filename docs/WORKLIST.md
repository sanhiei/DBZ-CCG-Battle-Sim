## Bundles, ranked by what a real game actually hits

### 1. The turn loop: four steps that do nothing (and one that does everything twice) _(large)_

This is the only bundle that fires every single turn for both players regardless of deck, and three of its four items are complete no-ops. Hands grow without bound (3 cards a Draw Step plus 3 more in every Prepare Phase, never trimmed), so a defence card is always in hand and attacks rarely land; personalities sit at the top of their ladder every turn, which inflates every PAT lookup and neuters attrition; and the attacker can never decline Combat, so the defender is handed 3 free cards every turn and the whole defensive/rebuild line of play does not exist.

- #4 PARTIAL — Power-Up applies twice per turn. packages/engine/src/reducer.ts:92 runs powerUp() on step entry without setting state.poweredUpThisTurn; the guard at reducer.ts:105-114 therefore lets one more full power-up through, and packages/client/src/components/Board.tsx:207 enables exactly that button in exactly that step. MP climbs 2x PUR, Allies climb 2 stages instead of 1. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- #2 OPEN — Discard Step is a pass-through. No hand limit exists anywhere: reducer.ts:90-93 has no 'discard' branch and a repo-wide grep for handLimit/HAND_LIMIT/'all but 1' across engine, shared and server returns nothing. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS. Needs a prompt (CRD 240-241: attacker discards to 1, then opponent does the same).
- #5 OPEN — Declare Step is not a decision. turn.ts:47-48/65 walks declare -> combat unconditionally; the only bypass is turn.ts:59 keyed on skipCombatThisTurn, written solely at noncombat.ts:83. No declareCombat action exists in packages/shared/src/protocol.ts:9-31. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- #25 OPEN — Rejuvenation Step is a pass-through. No 'rejuvenation' branch in reducer.ts:90-93; rejuvenate() at abilities.ts:151-162 is correct but reachable only as a card effect. Its precondition (did not declare Combat) is unrepresentable until #5 lands, though the Location/Battleground path is already recordable today.

**Unlocks:** Card economy becomes a real constraint, which is the precondition for every combat fix downstream mattering. Declining Combat makes the Rejuvenation entitlement reachable and gives Locations/Battlegrounds their intended payoff. Correct power stages make the PAT numbers in bundle 2 meaningful.

**Approach:** Do the one-line power-up fix first (set state.poweredUpThisTurn in reducer.ts:92) — ten minutes, and it corrects every PAT bracket in the game. Then add declaredCombat to GameState + a declareCombat action, widen the turn.ts:59 redirect, and build the discard and rejuvenation prompts on the existing pendingPrompt machinery. Migration cost: the fast-forward loops in engine.test.ts:85/99/134 spin `while (step !== 'combat') advanceStep` and will hang unless a declareCombat is injected.

### 2. Attack declaration: source, cost, and when the PAT is read _(large)_

Every attack in every game passes through here. Today the dominant line is 'press Physical attack, spend nothing, keep your whole hand for defence' — deck construction and card economy stop mattering on the offensive side exactly as they do on the hand-size side. The base-damage timing bug is worse than it looks: the control window only opens when the defender's MP is at its bottom two stages, i.e. precisely when the PAT gap is widest, so a defender is punished for using the rule correctly and the excess overflows into life cards that can cross the 5-card capture threshold.

- #3 OPEN — an attack can be declared with no card, no power and no cost, unlimited times. combat.ts:214-234 validates a card only `if (cardUid)`; nothing is spent (the spend helper opens `if (!uid) return;` at combat.ts:478-479); Board.tsx:194-195 ships two bare buttons that send no cardUid. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- #7 OPEN — the energy attack's 2-stage cost is not enforced. combat.ts:258-263 discards loseStages' unpayable remainder and there is no affordability guard, so an attacker at stageIndex 0 attacks free forever. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- #9 OPEN — Physical Base Damage is computed at declaration (combat.ts:266-268) from the pre-announcement controller, before openControlWindow at combat.ts:277; resolveControlOfCombat writes only defenderControllerUid at combat.ts:348 and never recomputes. Naming a healthy Ally moves the damage but keeps the crushed MP's PAT number. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- #30 OPEN — Final Physical Attack unreachable. finalUsed is initialised (combat.ts:86) and guarded twice (combat.ts:228, :601) but never written; no action exists in protocol.ts. Land it with #3: the cardless attack is currently the illegitimate substitute for it.
- #34 OPEN — double/triple base damage parses silently with no needsReview flag and no multiplier term in the combat.ts:722 total (8 cards). Small, and it belongs in the same arithmetic.

**Unlocks:** Combat cards become a spent resource; Control of Combat becomes worth using; Final Physical Attack (the desperation line that closes games) becomes playable with its real cost and lockout.

**Approach:** Requiring a real attack source is the expensive part — 58 of 63 declareAttack call sites across the engine tests pass `undefined` as cardUid, so build a shared fixture helper that instantiates a stock Physical Combat card first. Move the computeBaseDamage call out of declareAttack into resolveDefense just above the combat.ts:722 total, re-reading both controllers (the attacker's rating can move too, abilities.ts:123-124), and keep a distinct flag for 'the card stated a fixed base' rather than overloading `baseDamage === undefined`.

### 3. Defence resolution: what a card played as a defence actually does _(large)_

The single most-used decision in combat. Roughly a third of the combat pool is still a universal 'stop anything' regardless of printed type, prevention cards nullify attacks instead of reducing them, and one Defense Shield can shut down a whole Combat Step. Net effect: attacks land far less often than the rules allow, and when a defence does fire, the wrong thing happens on both sides of it.

- #0 PARTIAL — the residue. combat.ts:638-658 still falls through to `atk.stopped = true` for any card the parser produced no ability for, with the attack-type match at combat.ts:631-637 never run (it sits inside the `stops.length > 0` branch). 442 combat-typed cards in data/cards.tts.enriched.json carry no parsed ability, 284 of them printing attack text. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS. Widen the scope: the refutation found the same path also upgrades a prevention-only defence (trigger 'defense', no stopAttack) to a full type-agnostic stop, which a text-based predicate would not catch. defenseEffects (combat.ts:907-915) also still hands attack-trigger effects to the defensive rider path.
- #10 OPEN — preventLifeCards has no consumer anywhere (emitted abilities.ts:452-453, typed shared/abilities.ts:50, read by nothing), so a prevent-N card cancels the attack outright, and because atk.stopped is set the attack is never successful — the attacker also loses the capture and every if-successful rider. CRD:340: an attack is successful even if it deals no damage.
- #11 OPEN — combat.ts:660-662 grants a combat-long lockout on any thisCombat stop without reading e.scope, so a one-shot Defense Shield ('stops the FIRST unstopped physical attack this Combat') ends the opponent's entire offense for the Combat Step. ~17-18 cards.
- #44 OPEN — that lockout is enforced at declaration (combat.ts:229-230), above setupAttackAbility, above the cost, above any spend. So secondary effects never resolve (CRD:387), 'if this attack is stopped' riders can never fire, the card is never spent, and cards printed as exceptions to a lockout (Orange Energy Discharge) are unplayable.
- #29 OPEN — deferred stops ('next phase', 'first successful') are filtered out at combat.ts:631 and the card is refused at every moment, so 9 cards are dead in hand. The parser also collapses 'stops X and stops X next phase' into a single deferred stop (abilities.ts:433 claims the whole sentence).
- #26 OPEN — Focused is stripped at abilities.ts:672-673 and used only for a needsReview note; no Effect or AttackInProgress field carries it, so the coverage test at combat.ts:632 lets a dual-type stop block the 62 cards printed to beat exactly that.

**Unlocks:** Attacks start landing at their real rate, which is what makes bundles 2 and 4 visible at the table. It also introduces the stop-vs-prevent distinction (CRD:306-307) that Endurance, Focused and the Dragon Ball threshold all depend on.

**Approach:** One sitting in resolveDefense. Add lifeCardPrevention to AttackInProgress and let a prevention-only card fall through to steps 7/8 instead of returning early; make defenseEffects return [] rather than falling back to attack-trigger abilities; run the type-match on the no-modelled-stop path using the printed type; guard the lockout write with `e.scope === 'all'`; move the lockout check from declaration into resolution as a stop applied to a performed attack. #29 needs a pendingStops registry on CombatState plus a parser split on ' and '. Note defence-economy.test.ts:132-140 deliberately locks the current pass-through in and will need rewriting.

### 4. Damage arithmetic: the terms that never get added _(large)_

With the real PAT loaded (data/pat.json, placeholder:false), Drill modifiers are frequently the largest single term in a physical attack, and nearly every deck runs Drills — so physical damage totals are wrong in essentially every game. The other three are silent partial resolutions: the card does something, just not the printed amount, so players will not notice they are being cheated.

- #6 OPEN — no code path anywhere reads either player's zones.inPlay to modify damage. combat.ts:722 is a pure sum of card-local terms, atk.modifiers is written only from the attack card (abilities.ts:113), and the 'constant' trigger (shared/abilities.ts:14) is executed by nothing. 85 Drills print a signed damage clause and every one is inert in both directions.
- #22 OPEN — '+N life cards of damage' is parsed to a bare flag (abilities.ts:377-383) with no Effect kind to carry it and no term in either damage path. 51 abilities lose the life-card half of their printed damage.
- #23 OPEN — an attack stating both life cards and power stages deals only one of the two, in opposite directions for physical and energy (abilities.ts:103-110 plus the early return at combat.ts:701-716). The parser was fixed; the executor still throws one half away.
- #39 OPEN — changeControllerStages clamps at 0 (abilities.ts:47-48) and drops the remainder, so a card-effect drain below zero discards no life cards (CRD:390). discardForEffect exists at damage.ts:89 with no production caller. 13 abilities affected, 6 of them on cards that do not say 'to a minimum of 0'.

**Unlocks:** Damage numbers you can trust, which is also what makes the 5-life-card Dragon Ball capture threshold and Survival pacing correct.

**Approach:** #6 is the large one and drives the shape: it needs qualifiers on damageStages ('yoursOnly' vs 'againstYou', attack type), new parser rules for the two printed shapes, a data re-bake, and a boardModifiers(state, ...) helper consulted at combat.ts:714 and :722 with a floor at 0. #22/#23/#39 are each an Effect-union addition plus a couple of executor lines and ride along cheaply once the plumbing is open.

### 5. Server authority, input validation and prompt identity _(medium)_

Honest frequency note: two friends will not send hand-crafted WebSocket messages, so most of this bundle is near-zero frequency at the table. It is ranked here anyway because the promptId bug is hit by an ordinary double-click and silently destroys a defender's Endurance decision, the moveCard crash takes down every game in the process, and every item is individually a few lines. Severity per unit of effort is the highest in the worklist.

- authorize() only rejects a NUMERIC playerIdx — packages/server/src/room.ts:280 `typeof named === 'number' && named !== seatIdx`, so `playerIdx: '1'` skips the check and JS array indexing resolves the string anyway. Verified exploits: drawCards for the opponent empties their Life Deck for an instant Survival win; concede with a spoofed idx makes the sender the winner; chat impersonates the opponent in the shared log. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS (the guard reads exactly as reported).
- moveCard validates neither toZone nor toPlayerIdx — reducer.ts:166 `state.players[action.toPlayerIdx ?? loc.playerIdx]!.zones[action.toZone].push(inst)`. An unknown zone or out-of-range seat throws an uncaught TypeError that propagates through room.ts:179 and the bare ws listener at index.ts:71, killing the whole process and every game in it. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- moveCard also reads and steals from zones the sender does not own — findInstance walks both players' zones, redact.ts:12 preserves the real uid on hidden cards, so one message at a time reveals the opponent's hand and Life Deck, and a loop over their deck ends the game by Survival. The room.ts:273-277 tabletop-assist exemption is about touching an opponent's card, not about defeating redaction.
- answerPrompt ignores action.promptId — reducer.ts:195-217 dispatches purely on state.pendingPrompt?.type. Because one action can close a prompt and open the next for the same player (resolveDefense -> resolveLifeCardDamage opens the endurance prompt at combat.ts:777), a double-clicked button silently spends the defender's Endurance card and forfeits the prevention. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS (promptId appears nowhere in engine or server).
- drawCards has no step, turn or count validation — reducer.ts:96 `draw(state, action.playerIdx, action.count)`. A player can draw 20 cards for their own seat during the opponent's turn, and authorize() passes it because the seat matches.
- setStage accepts a non-numeric stageIndex — turn.ts:149 Math.max(0, Math.min('x', n)) yields NaN, giving stageIndex NaN and currentRating undefined, which poisons every later PAT lookup, makes `stageIndex > MP_DOWN_STAGES` permanently false, and cannot be recovered by normal play. (The cross-seat targeting half is a documented deliberate exemption — fix only the type guard.)
- Dragon Ball capture is not gated on a pending capture prompt — combat.ts:870-872 checks only that an attack exists and the caller is the attacker; no `pendingPrompt?.type === 'capture'` check and no lifeCardsDealt >= 5 check, unlike every sibling resolver. The call also deletes the defender's open prompt and cancels the in-flight attack. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- chat text is unbounded and state.log is never trimmed (reducer.ts:227), and room.ts:243-265 re-serialises the whole log to every client on every action.
- senseiCardId survives the redaction that hides zones.sensei (redact.ts:29 vs the `{...p, zones}` spread at :33). Per CRD:88 the Sensei card is public, so delete the dead hiding rather than redacting the field.

**Unlocks:** The server can be exposed beyond a fully trusted LAN, and prompt answers stop being ambiguous — which matters more as bundles 1-3 add more prompts (discard, rejuvenation, declare, prevention).

**Approach:** Almost all one-liners: reject any playerIdx that is not a number equal to seatIdx; validate toZone against the ZONES tuple and toPlayerIdx against range; Number.isInteger on stageIndex; compare action.promptId to state.pendingPrompt.id; gate resolveCapture on the capture prompt; cap chat text; delete the sensei hiding. The uid-adjacency leak (identical cards get consecutive uids because buildLifeDeck creates all copies in one run before shuffling, setup.ts:59-65) is fixed by stamping uids after the shuffle.

### 6. Anger arithmetic and personality levels _(medium)_

5 anger advances a personality level and reaching the top level by anger is the Most Powerful Personality victory — so the game's fastest win condition currently arrives roughly twice as fast as printed, and the cards printed to shut anger down feed it instead. Anger cards are common enough that a typical game hits several of these.

- #14 OPEN — conditional 'instead' clauses are additive. pushAnger (abilities.ts:500-544) pushes one changeAnger per clause with no reference to CONDITIONAL_CLAUSE (declared abilities.ts:299, used only inside parseAttackDamage). Shipped data confirms it: Black Hug Maneuver [+2,+3] = a free personality level off one attack; Gohan's Temper, Empowerment!, Goku's Super Saiyan Catch all +3 instead of 1; Orange Thumbs Up strips 3 instead of 1. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS (the loop and the terminal push read exactly as reported).
- #15 OPEN — prohibition clauses are executed as anger changes. pushAnger has no negation test at all, and toNum's fallback of 1 supplies a phantom +1. Awful Abrasions / Severe Bruises / Terrible Wounds / Goku's Heart Disease zero the victim's anger then hand back +1; Blue Head Charge's 'your opponent cannot gain any anger' mis-targets as +1 to the ATTACKER; Red Commander nets 0; Goku's Farewell nets -4. The refutation pass found 30 such cards in the catalog, not the 7 the finding names.
- #47 OPEN (latent) — advanceLevel wipes anger unconditionally at turn.ts:165 even when the advance was not by 5 anger (CRD:526). One line, and it goes live the moment #24 or any card-effect advance lands.
- #24 OPEN — losing a personality level is not implemented at all: nothing decrements currentLevel anywhere, there is no Effect kind for it, and the affected cards fail silently (Roshi's/King Kai's Calming parse to no ability and sit inert with no 'resolve manually' notice; Majin Buu's Bicycle Kick's clause is swallowed as a delta-0 changeAnger that counts as resolved).

**Unlocks:** A believable clock on the MPPV race, and the printed counterplay against a runaway anger deck.

**Approach:** #14/#15/#47 are one sitting: a CONDITIONAL_CLAUSE skip and a negation guard inside pushAnger's per-clause loop (both must sit before the toZero branch at abilities.ts:528 so the legitimate 'lower to 0' half survives), a needsReview flag plumbed out to all three parseAbility call sites, one line at turn.ts:165, then re-bake data/cards.tts.enriched.json and data/cards.saiyan.enriched.json via enrich-tts.ts:472 and author.ts:37. #24 is a separate, larger piece (new Effect kind, loseLevel in turn.ts, Drill discard per CRD:636, anger deliberately untouched per CRD:526) — split it out if the sitting runs long.

### 7. Allies and Control of Combat _(medium)_

Ally decks are a mainstream archetype and #16 makes any Ally above level 1 self-destruct on the first Power-Up — it then sits at rating 0, so the PAT puts it in the bottom bracket forever and loseStages computes Math.min(n, 0) = 0, meaning redirected damage overflows entirely into life cards. Redirecting to an Ally, the main reason to field one, becomes strictly worse than eating the hit. #8 then quietly hands the wrong personality's rating to every subsequent physical attack.

- #16 OPEN — a level-2+ Ally collapses to power rating 0. reducer.ts:151-152 builds it with `levelCardIds: [inst.cardId]` but `currentLevel: per.level`, and currentRatings (turn.ts:88) reads `levelCardIds[currentLevel - 1]` -> undefined -> `?? [0]`. The Ally enters looking correct at 3 stages above 0 and is destroyed by its owner's very next Power-Up Step. 357 of 600 ally-capable personalities are level 2+, and the shipped 'Blue Allies' preset carries a level-2 Chiaotzu. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS.
- #8 OPEN — Control of Combat never returns to the MP at the end of Combat. endCombatStep (combat.ts:174) calls only releaseControlIfMpRecovered, which refuses while mp.stageIndex <= 1 (turn.ts:120-122) — exactly the condition required to take control in the first place. An Ally that covered on turn 1 is still the fighter on turn 9, and the attacker side is never re-prompted.
- #40 OPEN — 'raise your Main Personality to his highest power stage' heals whoever is in Control. abilities.ts:41 and :67 both resolve to the controller, and EffectTarget is only 'user'|'foe' so the MP-vs-controller subject cannot be expressed. ~46 cards, and they exist precisely for the MP-down-with-an-Ally-covering situation they get wrong. Compounds with #8: because the flag never clears, an Ally can hold control during the Non-Combat Step too.
- #36 OPEN — overlaying an Ally pushes a second PersonalityInPlay instead of stacking a level (reducer.ts:151/158), sets it 3 above 0 instead of its new level's top stage, and rejects a level-2 Ally under a level-1 MP that CRD:554 explicitly permits.

**Unlocks:** Allies become playable at all above level 1, and the MP/Ally distinction (ratings, healing, control) starts behaving as printed.

**Approach:** #16 is a one-line clamp in currentRatings (index by `Math.min(currentLevel, levelCardIds.length) - 1`), which is a no-op for MPs and stays correct if #36's overlay later pushes onto levelCardIds — prefer it over setting currentLevel: 1, which forecloses overlay. #8 is an unconditional returnControlToMp called from endCombatStep, keeping the guarded release on the during-combat and Power-Up paths. #40 needs a `subject?: 'mp'` field on the two stage effects plus a data re-bake.

### 8. Non-Combat Step: timing and board legality _(large)_

Drills are continuous and their effects stack, so #18 lets a player table every Drill they draw plus duplicates — and once bundle 4 makes Drill damage modifiers actually apply, an unrestricted Drill board becomes the dominant strategy rather than an inert one. #17 removes all timing control from a whole card class and makes the starburst defence unreachable.

- #17 OPEN — Non-Combat cards fire their effect the instant they are played and are spent immediately (noncombat.ts:94-113, inside the `step !== 'nonCombat'` guard), so all 88 non-Drill Non-Combat cards with an onPlay ability can never be held. 'A Burst of Energy' resolves before your own Power-Up Step; a starburst card can never be on the table to defend with, even though combat.ts:453 already accepts a defence from zone 'inPlay'. Note onplay.test.ts:126-156 actively locks the wrong behaviour in.
- #18 OPEN — no Drill legality at all. playCard's only gate is the card-type check at noncombat.ts:62-64; isFreestyleDrill (drills.ts:33-37) has no production caller. Any mix of Styles and any number of duplicates go on the table simultaneously (159 Styled Drills). The refutation adds that styleOf reads only card.style, which is null for 9 Styled Drills, so even a Mastery deck's Tokui-Waza check misses them.
- #19 OPEN — Locations/Battlegrounds are pushed unconditionally at noncombat.ts:79: no single-in-play rule, no removal of the incumbent from the game, no same-name check, and the opponent's is never displaced. 16 Locations + 17 Battlegrounds, all continuous and affecting both players.

**Unlocks:** Drills and Locations become a real deckbuilding constraint rather than free accumulation; reactive Non-Combat cards become holdable, which is what CRD Attack Phase option 3 and defence option 2 are for.

**Approach:** #19 and #18 are small and independent — do them first (a uniqueness scan across both players' inPlay before the push, and a drillStyleOf derived from title OR the style field, per the refutation's correction). #17 is the large one: split noncombat.ts:45-117 so playCard is pure entry-into-play, add a `useCard` action gated on the Attack Phase, and rewrite the five onplay.test.ts cases that assert immediate resolution.

### 9. Dragon Ball identity and the victory check _(medium)_

A player can win outright without a real set, and a player who legitimately collects seven balls across two printings can never win — and capture, the primary route, misses identically. Both outcomes are silent and unrecoverable. #46 can hand someone a Survival loss they did not earn. Frequency is lower than the combat bundles because it needs a ball deck, but when it fires it decides the game.

- #20 OPEN — a 'set' is the printing's expansion saga and a ball's identity is its card NAME (victory.ts:49-50), with the same grouping duplicated in resolveCapture (combat.ts:879-887) and a third saga-based copy in decks.ts:176-183. Every shipped ball has number: null. Live consequences, all reproduced by two independent passes: 6 Alt. Dende balls + Earth Dragon Ball 3 (all saga 'Buu') is a FALSE WIN; a genuine Earth set split across the 'Unknown' and 'Saiyan' printings NEVER wins and never even defers on capture; and a CRD-legal Dende + Alt. Dende deck is rejected at deck build.
- #46 OPEN — isDragonBall (damage.ts:36-40) trusts rules.type only, and tts-9f900382-12 'Upa, Littlefoot' is mistyped 'Dragon Ball' in the catalog. In a Life Deck it can never be discarded as damage and cycles forever, which reports unpayable damage and triggers a FALSE Survival loss at combat.ts:797-800; played, it counts as an eighth ball toward the win.

### 10. Deck construction legality _(medium)_

Everything here is decided once at deck build and then shapes every turn of the game. #27 in particular means a player draws a limit-3 combat card twice as often as the rules allow, all game, with no visible tell. #41's +1 PUR compounds directly with bundle 1's power-up fix.

- #35 OPEN — playAlly gates level only (reducer.ts:141-143). Alignment is copied at reducer.ts:150 and never compared, canBeAlly is loaded and read by no engine code, and personalityName is never compared to the MP's. A Hero Goku deck fields Nappa and a second Goku, in play and in deck validation. VERIFIED IN THIS PASS. Data caveat: canBeAlly is hardcoded true for all 600 personalities, so that clause needs curated data before it has teeth; alignment (326 Hero / 237 Villain / 37 Rogue) is usable today. Compare against p.alignment, not the current level card's — the shipped 'Blue Allies' MP mixes alignments across its level cards.
- #27 PARTIAL — copy limits are counted per printing, not per title. decks.ts:142-147 keys on name|saga|level; dropping saga is a one-line fix. 205 non-Personality titles have 2+ printings, so 6 copies of a limit-3 card or 4 of a limit-1 Mastery are legal today. cheap-fixes.test.ts:160-175 currently asserts the wrong behaviour and must be inverted.
- #42 OPEN — the deck-construction rule that an Ally must be 2 levels below the MP's highest printed level is enforced nowhere; only the weaker play-time rule (maxAllyLevel = mp.currentLevel) exists, and it loosens as the MP advances.
- #43 OPEN — 31 catalog entries print 'Sensei Deck only' and nothing anywhere reads that phrase; they are accepted in the Life Deck. Sequence with #37 or gate it behind DeckValidationOptions, since until the Sensei Deck is materialised the check makes those cards unplayable rather than misplaced.
- #41 OPEN — Saiyan/Namekian Tokui-Waza is granted on Mastery style alone (mastery.ts:48-83) with no Heritage check on the MP, and decks.ts:198 raises the deck ceiling to 90 the same way. That is +1 PUR all game (turn.ts:83) plus Endurance (damage.ts:149) for a deck not entitled to them.
- #37 OPEN — the Sensei Deck never exists: deck.senseiDeck validates then evaporates (setup.ts:95-98 instantiates only the Sensei card itself), and there is no action, step or phase in which the setup-step-6 swap could happen. Large; split out.

**Unlocks:** Decks that are actually legal, which is the precondition for any balance judgement about the other bundles. Note the alignment check will make the shipped 'Blue Allies' preset illegal (Villain MP, seven Hero Allies), so the presets need rebuilding alongside it.

**Approach:** #27 and #42 are one-liners in decks.ts; #43 is a regex plus a loop over deck.life only (not the merged counts map). #35 is a few checks duplicated in reducer.ts and validateDeck. #41 wants the CRD:908-948 rosters transcribed into two frozen Sets in mastery.ts — smaller and safer than a data migration. Leave #37 for its own sitting.

### 11. Personality powers, Constant Combat Powers and the Prepare Phase _(large)_

Every Main Personality and Ally in the game is currently a stat block with inert text, and one of the two legal ways to spend an Attack Phase (using a Personality Power to attack) does not exist. Decks built around a signature power play as blank bodies. It is ranked below the mechanical bundles only because the fix is gated on a large data/parser effort rather than because it matters less.

- #12 OPEN — no personality card has an ability at all: enrich-tts.ts:471 explicitly skips ability parsing for type Personality, so 0 of 601 personalities carry abilities despite all 601 having rules.text. There is no action to invoke a power, no once-per-turn tracking on PersonalityInPlay, no reset on a level change, and no MP-vs-Ally exclusion. 'personalityPower' is a trigger produced and consumed by nothing.
- #13 OPEN — Constant Combat Powers have no continuous layer. The 'constant' trigger (shared/abilities.ts:14) is read by no source file; 209 of 601 personalities print one. The CRD:495 rule that a constant power switches off while another personality holds Control is also unrepresented.
- #28 OPEN — the Prepare Phase runs only the defender's draw (combat.ts:76-89). Neither player's 'When entering Combat' half exists, and the whenEnteringCombat trigger declared at shared/abilities.ts:13 is emitted by no parser branch and consumed by nothing. 149 cards print that text and do nothing all game. - Related: the Defense Shields printed on personalities (Android 18, Android 16, Vegeta Ascendant…) never fire at step 7, because activateDefenseShields (combat.ts:386-417) scans only zones.inPlay — CRD defence option 3 is unreachable.

**Approach:** Sequence: parser first (a personality branch in enrich-tts.ts splitting the power box on 'Constant Combat Power:' vs a leading 'Power:', plus a whenEnteringCombat branch in parseAbility — note parseAbility returns a single Ability today and must return a list, which ripples into loader consumers). ### 12. Empower, Dragon Ball capture routes, and the deep-content tail _(large)_

These are whole mechanics that are absent rather than wrong, and each needs data or parser work before any engine change means anything. Ranked last because a game between two friends mostly just never sees them — but the Empower pair is worth pulling forward if either of you runs Empower cards, since it is simultaneously a dead mechanic and, the moment a declaration path exists, an instant-win hole.

- Empower is unreachable AND unvalidated — two halves of one problem. No client can ever declare it (zero 'empower' hits across packages/client/src; 12 send sites and none sends declareEmpower, and no 'empower' prompt is ever raised even though shared/state.ts:193 declares the type) — VERIFIED IN THIS PASS — so atk.empower is permanently undefined and every 'Empower X' card plays as if the keyword were absent. Meanwhile #21: declareEmpower (combat.ts:355-361) validates nothing — no card lookup, no printed Empower Number anywhere in the data (67 cards print one, 0 carry a parsed value), no step gate — so a crafted `{amount: 99}` wins on the spot, and the text the Empower is supposed to delete (CRD:1102) still resolves.
- #31 OPEN — the Personality Capture rule (battle-sequence step 11) is not implemented; resolutionStep never takes the value 11, and canCaptureDragonBall is stamped by the scraper and read by nobody. Cheap-Ally capture decks cannot capture at all.
- #32 OPEN — card captures are impossible: resolveCapture requires an in-progress attack owned by the caller, but currentAttack is deleted at combat.ts:163 as soon as the attack resolves, and there is no capture Effect kind for the parser to emit. Every 'capture a Dragon Ball' card is dead text.
- #33 OPEN — a ball's powers are never used, on play (noncombat.ts:72-77 returns before the onPlay lookup) or on capture, and 0 of 53 balls carry a parsed ability, so wiring the path alone would execute nothing. Mostly data work.
- #38 OPEN — no victory can be postponed or prohibited, and MPPV is a one-tick edge trigger (victory.ts:124-130) whose anger signal dies with the action, so a deferred win would never re-fire.
- #48 OPEN — discardForEffect (damage.ts:89) has no production caller and discardCards always takes from the hand, so the Dragon Ball non-damage discard rules (CRD:695/703) are implemented and dead. Latent today; no shipped card reaches it.

**Unlocks:** The Empower keyword, both CRD capture routes (so a ball can change hands other than on a single 5+ life-card hit), and the postponement machinery the Most Powerful Personality victory needs.

**Approach:** Do the Empower pair as one job — the printed Empower Number has to be extracted into EngineCardRules during enrichment before either the UI affordance or the validation is meaningful, and the declaration window must move ahead of setupAttackAbility so the post-Empower text can be skipped. The capture items depend on bundle 9's ballIdentity helper and on step 14 being anchored correctly, so land bundle 9 first.

## Recommendation

Start with bundle 1 (the turn loop), and inside it do the power-up one-liner before anything else: setting `state.poweredUpThisTurn = true` alongside the automatic power-up at packages/engine/src/reducer.ts:92 is a single line that stops every personality in the game climbing at twice its printed rate, and every PAT lookup, every damage total and every attrition judgement downstream depends on the scouters being in the right place. Then do the Discard Step, because an unbounded hand is what currently makes the defence side of combat unfalsifiable — a defender always has a card, so no amount of work on bundles 2-4 will visibly change how a game plays until hands are trimmed. Declare and Rejuvenation complete the loop and are the cheapest way to make Locations, Battlegrounds and the whole skip-combat line real. Bundle 1 is the only bundle where every item fires every turn for both players, and it is the prerequisite for being able to tell whether anything else you fix actually worked.

Two deviations worth making deliberately rather than by accident. First, if this server is ever going to accept a connection from outside a fully trusted LAN, promote bundle 5 ahead of everything — it contains a one-message instant win (room.ts:280) and a one-message process kill (reducer.ts:166), both fixable in an afternoon. Second, regardless of ordering, pull the `answerPrompt` promptId check out of bundle 5 and do it with bundle 1: bundles 1-3 all add new prompts, and a double-clicked button silently eating the defender's Endurance decision is a bug two friends will actually hit and will not understand.

After bundle 1, work down 2 -> 3 -> 4 in order — declaration, defence, then damage arithmetic — because that is the order the battle sequence resolves in and each one makes the next observable. Do not start bundle 4's Drill modifiers before bundle 8's Drill legality is at least scoped: making 85 inert Drills live on an unrestricted board where a player can table every Drill they draw, in any mix of Styles, in triplicate, would swing the game further from the CRD than leaving them inert does today.

## Caveats

Verification scope: I re-read and confirmed with my own eyes the current source for the items marked "VERIFIED IN THIS PASS" — reducer.ts:90-115 (auto power-up sets no flag), reducer.ts:151-152 + turn.ts:87-90 (ally ladder), reducer.ts:162-168 (moveCard), reducer.ts:193-217 (promptId never read), combat.ts:214-285 (declareAttack: no source required, cost discarded, base damage cached pre-control-window), combat.ts:488-535 (spend, permanent arm skips removal-from-game), combat.ts:620-680 (defence fallback and lockout write), combat.ts:865-900 (resolveCapture ungated, saga-keyed set), turn.ts:44-105 (advanceStep, currentRatings, moveStage), abilities.ts:528-544 (pushAnger, no negation and no conditional guard), room.ts:265-285 (authorize), plus negative greps proving no hand-limit code and no client Empower path. Everything else I took from the two independent verification passes, which agreed with each other and whose sampled citations all resolved correctly when I checked them.

What I did NOT do: I ran no runtime probes of my own and did not re-run the test suite in this pass, so all runtime reproductions, all catalog counts (442 unparsed combat cards, 51 lifeCardModifier abilities, 30 negated-anger cards, 149 entering-Combat cards, 357 level-2+ allies, 205 multi-printing titles, 53 typed Dragon Balls, 31 "Sensei Deck only" entries) and all CRD line numbers are inherited from those passes rather than independently re-derived.

Ranking is judgement, not measurement. I ordered by how often two friends playing normally would hit each item, then by how badly it distorts the game. That deliberately pushes the server-authority bundle to rank 5 despite containing the two most severe defects in the list (instant win, process crash), because friendly players will not send crafted messages — the two items in it that DO fire accidentally are the promptId double-click and, if a manual-adjust UI ever sends a bad value, the setStage NaN. If this server is ever exposed beyond a trusted LAN, promote bundle 5 to rank 1 without hesitation.

Two known couplings that constrain ordering: #25 (Rejuvenation) cannot be fully correct until #5 (Declare) records the decision; #43 ("Sensei Deck only") makes 31 cards unplayable rather than misplaced until #37 materialises the Sensei Deck. And two items in the stale file will fight the fix: defence-economy.test.ts:132-140 and cheap-fixes.test.ts:160-175 both assert current wrong behaviour as intended and must be inverted, not preserved.

One unattributed gap neither the old audit nor the new sweep lists as its own finding: activateDefenseShields (combat.ts:386-417) scans only zones.inPlay, so the Defense Shields printed on personality cards never fire at step 7 at all. I folded it into bundle 11 because it arrives with Personality Powers, but it is not tracked anywhere as a finding.
